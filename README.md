# Awesome-Nonprofit-Grant-Management

## Top Grant Management (Nonprofit) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Grantmaking, Grant Lifecycle Management, Application Intake, Review Workflows, Compliance, Reporting & Nonprofit Funding Operations*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Grant Management** in the nonprofit and foundation sector. These systems help grantmakers and grantseekers manage the full lifecycle—from opportunity discovery and application intake through review, award, disbursement, reporting, and compliance.



**Examples** include Foundant Grant Lifecycle Manager, SmartSimple, Fluxx, Good Grants, AmpliFund, Submittable, Blackbaud Grantmaking, WizeHive Zengine, eCivis, and Optimy (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured grant management platforms with complex review workflows, compliance tracking, and multi-program portfolio tools are predominantly commercial. Open-source activity includes Microsoft’s Nonprofit Grant Management accelerator, ERPNext-based grant apps, membership/association tools that can be extended, and emerging full-lifecycle experiments. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Fluxx](https://www.fluxx.io/)**  

  Leading enterprise grantmaking platform built specifically for foundations and grantmakers, covering configurable intake, multi-stage review, awards, payments, compliance, and portfolio reporting.



- **[Foundant Grant Lifecycle Manager (GLM)](https://www.foundant.com/)**  

  Widely adopted grant management system for foundations, supporting the full lifecycle from application through reporting, outcomes, and compliance—especially strong for mid-size to large portfolios.



- **[SmartSimple](https://www.smartsimple.com/)**  

  Highly configurable platform used for grants, research funding, and complex multi-stakeholder workflows, with strong process automation capabilities.



- **[Submittable](https://www.submittable.com/)**  

  Flexible application intake and review platform popular with foundations, arts organizations, and programs that need customizable forms and evaluation workflows.



- **[Blackbaud Grantmaking, AmpliFund, Good Grants](https://www.blackbaud.com/)**  

  Solutions spanning grantmaking, grant management for recipients, and specialized funding program administration within broader nonprofit ecosystems.



- **[WizeHive Zengine, eCivis, Optimy](https://www.wizehive.com/)**  

  Additional platforms supporting grants, scholarships, government funding, and impact/program management with configurable workflows.



- **[Other grant management & nonprofit funding platforms](https://www.fluxx.io/)**  

  Commercial tools covering discovery, compliance (including federal/OMB requirements), and outcomes reporting for grantmakers and grantees.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Microsoft Nonprofit Grant Management](https://github.com/microsoft/Nonprofits)**  

  Open-source accelerator / model-driven app built on Microsoft Power Platform and the Common Data Model for Nonprofits. Provides a foundation for managing grant requests, awards, disbursements, reporting, and constituent data.



- **[Navari Grants Management (ERPNext)](https://github.com/navariltd/navari_gms)**  

  Open-source grants management app for ERPNext aimed at nonprofits—covering grant calls, proposal evaluation, awarding, monitoring, closeout, and analytical reporting.



- **[Emerging full-lifecycle grant management experiments](https://github.com/search?q=grant+management+OR+grantmaking+open+source)**  

  Community and initiative projects exploring AI-assisted prospecting, application intake, review workflows, and compliance tracking for grantmakers and grantseekers.



- **[Galette](https://github.com/galette/galette)**  

  Open-source membership and contribution management system designed for nonprofits and associations; can be extended or integrated for basic funding and contribution tracking.



- **[Common Data Model for Nonprofits & related schemas](https://github.com/microsoft/Nonprofits)**  

  Open data models and schemas that support interoperability of nonprofit and grant-related data across systems.



- **[CRM & case-management open tools adapted for grants](https://github.com/search?q=nonprofit+CRM+OR+case+management+open+source)**  

  Open CRM and case systems that organizations customize for grantee relationship management and simple award tracking.



- **[Form & workflow engines](https://github.com/search?q=form+builder+OR+workflow+engine+open+source)**  

  Self-hosted form builders and workflow tools used to create custom application and review processes.



- **[Reporting & impact measurement open tools](https://github.com/search?q=impact+measurement+OR+outcomes+reporting+nonprofit)**  

  Community projects focused on outcomes tracking and basic grant reporting that can complement core management systems.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **ERPNext / Odoo nonprofit modules**: Broader open ERP systems with accounting, project, and custom doctype support that some organizations adapt for grant tracking.

- **CiviCRM**: Open-source CRM popular in the nonprofit sector; can be extended with custom fields and workflows for grant-related processes.

- **Survey & application forms**: LimeSurvey and similar tools for structured intake when full GMS is not yet needed.

- **Document & evidence management**: Open document stores with review/approval workflows for proposals and reports.

- **Local AI / privacy-focused assistants**: Experimental tools that keep grant data and AI processing on-premises.

- Lightweight stacks: Custom forms + CRM + spreadsheets/airtable-style open tools for very small grant programs.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

There is no mature, widely adopted open-source equivalent to enterprise platforms such as Fluxx, Foundant GLM, or SmartSimple.  

The most concrete starting points are **Microsoft’s Nonprofit Grant Management** accelerator (Power Platform) and **ERPNext-based grant apps**.  

**CiviCRM**, **Galette**, and open form/workflow tools can support lighter or highly customized needs.  

Commercial platforms dominate for complex review committees, compliance (especially government/federal), payment integration, and multi-program portfolio management.  

Many smaller foundations and nonprofits begin with spreadsheets or lightweight tools and adopt commercial GMS as volume and compliance requirements grow; open-source components are most useful for data models, internal prototypes, or integration layers.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Grant management involves sensitive financial, personal, and sometimes government-regulated data. Proper access controls, audit trails, data protection, and compliance with funder and legal requirements are essential.

- Open-source tools and accelerators offer transparency and customization potential but typically require significant configuration, hosting, and ongoing maintenance. Evaluate security, accessibility, and total cost of ownership carefully before production use in grantmaking operations.
